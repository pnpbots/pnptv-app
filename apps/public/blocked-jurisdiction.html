Why is PNPtv restricted here?

PNPtv is an adult content platform. Several US states and the United Kingdom have enacted laws requiring adult platforms to collect, verify, and retain government-issued identification for every user. PNPtv has chosen not to collect that data — protecting user privacy is a core platform value. Rather than comply with these identification mandates, we block access from those jurisdictions entirely.

This is a temporary measure. PNPtv is exploring privacy-preserving age verification solutions (such as zero-knowledge proofs) that would allow access without identity exposure. When that system is available, affected users will be notified. See 18 U.S.C. § 2257 records for compliance documentation.
