Service Not Available in Your Jurisdiction

We're not able to serve users from your location due to local laws.

Why this page?

PNPtv is a private, members-only platform for the queer PNP community, incorporated and operated under the laws of Colombia. We've geo-blocked the following jurisdictions because their local laws would require us to either:

  • Collect and store sensitive government identification data (creating a security risk for our members);
  • Re-verify identity at frequent intervals and retain user data for years;
  • Submit to fines or penalties incompatible with the privacy expectations of our community.

Currently blocked: Texas, Tennessee, and Florida (USA), and the United Kingdom.

What can you do?

If you believe this block is in error (you're traveling, your VPN routes through a blocked region, etc.), or if you'd like to learn more about the laws involved:

  • Email us at support@pnptv.app with details — we review case-by-case.
  • Read the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • If you're in the UK and want to learn more about the Online Safety Act: ofcom.org.uk.

Our position

We believe the right balance is members-only privacy with strong age affirmation, not centralized ID collection that creates honeypots for hackers and outing risks for queer and PNP-community members. Several US states and the UK disagree, and we respect their authority within their borders by not serving users there.

This is a constant evolution. If the laws change, this page will too.
