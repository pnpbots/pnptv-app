Community Guidelines

Last updated: March 4, 2026

PNPtv is an 18+ adult community platform built for the queer PNP community. These guidelines exist to keep our space welcoming, safe, and respectful for all members. By using PNPtv, you agree to follow these guidelines. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban.

1. Age Requirement

PNPtv is strictly an 18+ platform. You must be at least 18 years of age to create an account, access content, or participate in any community features. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy regarding minors.

  • All users must be 18 or older. No exceptions.
  • Any content depicting, referencing, or involving minors in any sexual or suggestive context will result in immediate permanent ban and reporting to the appropriate authorities.
  • Users who are discovered to be underage will have their accounts immediately terminated.
  • If you suspect a user is underage, report them immediately using the in-app reporting tools or by emailing support@pnptv.app.

2. Respect and Inclusivity

PNPtv is a space for the queer PNP community. We expect all members to treat each other with dignity and respect, regardless of race, ethnicity, nationality, gender identity, sexual orientation, body type, HIV status, disability, or any other personal characteristic.

  • Use people's preferred names and pronouns.
  • Do not engage in discrimination, bigotry, or hate speech of any kind.
  • Respect boundaries. "No" always means no, online and offline.
  • Do not shame, stigmatize, or judge other community members for their choices, lifestyle, or status.
  • Welcome newcomers and help maintain a positive environment.

3. No Harassment or Threats

Harassment, intimidation, and threatening behavior are strictly prohibited on PNPtv.

  • No harassment: Do not repeatedly send unwanted messages, follow, or target any user after they have asked you to stop or blocked you.
  • No doxxing: Never share another person's private information (real name, address, phone number, workplace, photos) without their explicit consent. This applies both on and off the platform.
  • No threats: Threats of violence, harm, or any form of intimidation will result in immediate account termination and may be reported to law enforcement.
  • No blackmail or extortion: Attempting to coerce, blackmail, or extort any community member is grounds for immediate permanent ban and legal action.
  • No stalking: Do not track, follow, or monitor another user's activity in a way that makes them feel unsafe.

4. Consent is Mandatory

All interactions on PNPtv, whether in live video rooms, direct messages, or community spaces, must be consensual.

  • Always obtain clear, affirmative consent before engaging in intimate or sexual interactions.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time. Respect it immediately.
  • Do not record, screenshot, or redistribute content from private conversations, video calls, or live rooms without the explicit consent of all participants.
  • Do not share or distribute intimate content of another person without their consent (revenge content is a permanent ban offense).

5. Prohibited Content and Activities

The following are strictly prohibited on PNPtv:

  • Minor-related content: Any sexual or suggestive content involving minors. Zero tolerance, immediate permanent ban, and reporting to authorities.
  • Non-consensual content: Any content depicting sexual acts without the consent of all parties involved.
  • Trafficking: Any content or activity related to human trafficking, sexual exploitation, or forced labor.
  • Substance-related conduct: While PNPtv is a queer PNP community, the platform may NOT be used to:
    • Sell, distribute, traffic, or facilitate the sale of any illegal substance — anywhere on the platform.
    • Ask how or where to obtain illegal substances. No "where can I get…", no "DM me to score", no sourcing requests.
    • Tell, teach, or instruct others on how to obtain, manufacture, or synthesize illegal substances.
    Discussions about personal use in the context of harm reduction are permitted (hydration, pacing, knowing your limits, drug interactions awareness, after-care). When in doubt, frame the conversation as "what kept me safe" not "what I used and where I got it."
  • Props vs. real substances in videos: Videos uploaded to PNPtv often depict substance use in PNP/chemsex contexts. Creators and members are required to declare whether what is shown is a prop (theatrical / staged) or a real substance. PNPtv encourages prop use for performance content. When real substances are shown, the depiction must comply with all rules above (no sourcing, no instruction, harm-reduction framing). Failure to declare on request from a moderator may result in content removal.
  • Fraud and scams: Do not use PNPtv to scam, defraud, or mislead other users. This includes fake profiles, catfishing, and financial schemes.
  • Violence: Content that glorifies or promotes real-world violence, terrorism, or self-harm.
  • Impersonation: Do not impersonate other users, public figures, or PNPtv staff.
  • Spam: Do not spam chat rooms, direct messages, or community feeds with repetitive, irrelevant, or promotional content.

6. Content Sharing Rules

When sharing content on PNPtv (photos, videos, posts, live streams):

  • You must have the right to share any content you post. Do not post copyrighted material without permission.
  • All individuals appearing in your content must be 18+ and must have given their consent to be depicted.
  • Use appropriate content tags and categories so other users can filter content according to their preferences.
  • Do not share content from other users' profiles, live rooms, or private messages without their explicit permission.
  • Respect the privacy of live room participants. What happens in a room stays in the room unless all parties consent to sharing.

7. Live Video Rooms

Live video rooms are a core feature of PNPtv. Additional rules apply:

  • Room hosts are responsible for maintaining a respectful environment within their rooms.
  • Follow any room-specific rules set by the host.
  • Do not disrupt rooms with unwanted behavior, spam, or harassment.
  • Do not record or stream the contents of a live room to external platforms without explicit consent from all visible participants.
  • Hosts have the right to remove participants who violate room rules or these community guidelines.

8. Reporting

If you encounter behavior or content that violates these guidelines, please report it immediately. We take all reports seriously and review them promptly.

  • In-App Reporting: Use the report button available on user profiles, content posts, messages, and live rooms.
  • Email: Send detailed reports to support@pnptv.app with relevant screenshots, usernames, and descriptions.
  • Emergency: If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first, then report to us.

All reports are confidential. We do not reveal the identity of reporters to the reported user. False or malicious reporting is itself a violation of these guidelines.

9. Enforcement and Consequences

Violations of these Community Guidelines will be handled through a tiered enforcement system:

  • First Offense (Minor): Written warning with an explanation of the violation and a reminder of community standards.
  • Second Offense or Moderate Violation: Temporary suspension (24 hours to 30 days depending on severity), with content removal if applicable.
  • Serious Violation: Immediate temporary suspension (7-30 days), content removal, and account review.
  • Severe Violation: Immediate permanent ban. This includes any content involving minors, non-consensual content, doxxing, threats of violence, trafficking, or repeated serious violations.

PNPtv reserves the right to skip tiers and impose immediate permanent bans for severe violations. Banned users may not create new accounts. Attempts to circumvent a ban will result in further action.

Users who are permanently banned may appeal by emailing legal@pnptv.app with "Ban Appeal" in the subject line. Appeals are reviewed within 30 days.

10. Your Role in the Community

PNPtv is built by and for our community. Every member plays a role in making this space safer and more welcoming. We encourage you to:

  • Lead by example with respectful, consensual interactions.
  • Report violations when you see them.
  • Support fellow community members, especially newcomers.
  • Engage in open, honest dialogue while respecting differences.
  • Look out for each other, both online and offline.

11. Wellness, Sobriety, and Harm Reduction Resources

PNPtv is built around the queer PNP community, and we take care of our own. We provide the following resources directly inside the platform:

  • Sobriety & Recovery Hangouts: Members-only Hangout groups for people in recovery, considering sobriety, or who want a sober space within the community. No judgment, no relapse-shaming. Find them in the Hangouts directory under "Sobriety" or ask Cristina AI.
  • Harm-Reduction Educational Library: Articles and short videos on safer-use practices, drug interactions, hydration and rest, recognizing overdose signs, naloxone access, HIV/STI prevention (PrEP, doxyPEP), and aftercare. Available from Cristina's profile and the Resources section.
  • Cristina AI: Our virtual community guide can answer questions about safer-use practices, refer you to recovery resources, and help you find sober Hangouts. She does NOT provide instructions on substance use itself — her role is harm reduction and connection to support.
  • External Help: If you are in crisis or need immediate support: SAMHSA National Helpline (US): 1-800-662-4357 (free, 24/7, confidential). Crystal Meth Anonymous: crystalmeth.org. The Trevor Project (LGBTQ+ crisis): 1-866-488-7386. International: findahelpline.com.

Asking for help — or offering it to a friend in the community — is welcomed and encouraged here. You are not alone.

12. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these Community Guidelines from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through platform notifications and/or email. Continued use of PNPtv after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated guidelines.
