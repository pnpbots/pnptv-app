General Support

For account issues, technical problems, or general questions:

Legal Inquiries

For legal matters, DMCA notices, law enforcement requests, or compliance questions:

Email: legal@pnptv.app

Payment & Billing

For subscription issues, refund requests, or payment problems:

Email: support@pnptv.app

Report Abuse

To report content violations, harassment, or safety concerns:

Email: support@pnptv.app

Use the in-app reporting tools for fastest response, or email with screenshots and details.

Telegram Support

You can also reach us through our Telegram bot. Open the PNPtv bot and use the /support command to submit a support request directly.
