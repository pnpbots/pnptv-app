Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how PNPtv ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit or use our platform at pnptv.app. By continuing to use our platform, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply information to the site operators. Cookies can be "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent" cookies (remaining on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

2. Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the operation of PNPtv. Without them, the platform cannot function properly. They cannot be disabled.

  • Authentication Cookies: We use session cookies to keep you logged in after you authenticate via Telegram, X (Twitter), Bluesky, or Email. These cookies store a secure session token and expire when you log out or after a period of inactivity.
  • Security Cookies: We use cookies to help detect and prevent fraudulent activity, unauthorized access, and cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks.
  • Load Balancing Cookies: These ensure your requests are routed to the same server during your session, providing a consistent experience.
  • Payment Session Cookies: When you make a payment through ePayco or Daimo, temporary cookies may be set to maintain the integrity of your payment transaction.

3. Functional / Preference Cookies

These cookies allow PNPtv to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, personalized features.

  • Language Preference: We store your preferred language (English or Spanish) so the platform displays in your chosen language on subsequent visits.
  • Theme Preference: Your display settings and UI preferences are saved so you do not need to reconfigure them each time you visit.
  • Video/Audio Settings: Your preferred video quality, audio volume, and streaming settings for live rooms are remembered.
  • Content Preferences: Cookies may store your content feed preferences and filter settings to deliver a tailored experience.

4. Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with PNPtv. This helps us improve the platform, identify popular features, and fix issues.

  • Usage Analytics: We collect anonymized data about page views, session duration, feature usage, and navigation patterns. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual users.
  • Performance Monitoring: Cookies help us measure page load times, error rates, and overall platform performance to ensure a smooth experience.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell any cookie data to external parties.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services that we integrate with:

  • Payment Processors: ePayco and Daimo may set their own cookies during the payment process to verify transactions and prevent fraud.
  • Authentication Providers: When you log in via Telegram, X (Twitter), or Bluesky, these services may set cookies on their own domains as part of the OAuth authentication flow.
  • Content Delivery: Our CDN may set cookies to optimize content delivery and load balancing.

We do not control third-party cookies. Please refer to the respective privacy policies of these services for more information.

6. Cookie Consent

When you first visit PNPtv, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or customize your cookie preferences. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for the platform to function. You may accept or decline functional and analytics cookies at any time.

Your consent preferences are stored in a cookie itself, so we can remember your choice on future visits. You can change your preferences at any time through your account settings or by clearing your browser cookies.

7. How to Manage Cookies

You have several options for managing cookies:

  • Browser Settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically find these in the "Options," "Settings," or "Preferences" menu of your browser. You can set your browser to block or delete cookies, though this may affect platform functionality.
  • Account Settings: You can manage your cookie preferences within your PNPtv account settings under the Privacy section.
  • Clearing Cookies: You can delete all cookies stored by PNPtv at any time by clearing your browser's cookie data. Note that this will log you out and reset your preferences.

8. Cookie Retention

The duration cookies remain on your device depends on their type:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted automatically when you close your browser.
  • Authentication Cookies: Persist for up to 30 days or until you log out.
  • Preference Cookies: Persist for up to 1 year.
  • Analytics Cookies: Persist for up to 1 year.
  • Consent Cookie: Persists for up to 1 year to remember your cookie preferences.

9. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting. PNPtv respects DNT signals. When we detect a DNT header, we will disable non-essential analytics cookies for that session.

We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
