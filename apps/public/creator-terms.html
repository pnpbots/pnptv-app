Creator & Performer Terms

Last updated: March 4, 2026

These Creator & Performer Terms govern the relationship between content creators/performers ("Creators") and PNPtv. By applying for and maintaining Creator status on PNPtv, you agree to these terms in addition to our Terms of Service, Content Policy, and Community Guidelines.

1. Eligibility

To become a Creator on PNPtv, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Age: You must be at least 18 years of age. No exceptions.
  • Identity Verification: You must complete our identity verification process, which may include providing a government-issued photo ID and a selfie for verification purposes. This is required to confirm your age and identity.
  • Application Approval: Creator applications are reviewed and approved by PNPtv. We reserve the right to approve or deny applications at our discretion.
  • Account Standing: You must have an active PNPtv account in good standing with no history of serious policy violations.
  • Legal Capacity: You must have the legal capacity to enter into a binding agreement in your jurisdiction.

PNPtv may require additional verification or documentation at any time to maintain Creator status.

2. Revenue Split

PNPtv operates on an 80/20 revenue split model:

  • Creator Share: 80% of all net revenue generated from your content, including subscription-based content, tips, pay-per-view content, and other monetization features.
  • Platform Share: 20% retained by PNPtv to cover platform operations, payment processing, hosting, moderation, and service development.

"Net revenue" means the gross amount paid by the user minus payment processing fees charged by our payment processors (ePayco, Daimo). Payment processing fees typically range from 2.5% to 5% depending on the payment method and region.

Example: If a user pays $10 for your content, and the payment processing fee is $0.50, the net revenue is $9.50. You receive $7.60 (80% of $9.50), and PNPtv retains $1.90 (20% of $9.50).

3. Payouts

Creator earnings are tracked in your Creator Dashboard and paid out according to the following schedule:

  • Payout Frequency: Payouts are processed on a bi-weekly basis (every two weeks), on the 1st and 15th of each month.
  • Minimum Payout: A minimum balance of $50 USD must be accumulated before a payout is processed. Balances below the minimum threshold carry over to the next payout period.
  • Payout Methods: Payouts are available via bank transfer, ePayco balance, or stablecoin transfer (USDC) to your designated wallet address.
  • Processing Time: Bank transfers may take 3-7 business days to arrive after processing. Stablecoin payouts are typically completed within 24 hours.
  • Payout Currency: Payouts are denominated in USD. If you receive payouts via bank transfer to a non-USD account, your bank's exchange rate will apply.
  • Holds: PNPtv may place a temporary hold on payouts (up to 30 days) for new Creators or when unusual activity is detected, to protect against fraud and chargebacks.

4. Tax Responsibility

Creators are independent contractors, not employees of PNPtv. As such:

  • Tax Obligations: You are solely responsible for reporting and paying all applicable taxes (income tax, VAT, sales tax, self-employment tax, etc.) on your Creator earnings in your jurisdiction.
  • Tax Forms: PNPtv may provide annual earnings summaries for your tax records. For US-based Creators earning above the applicable threshold, PNPtv will issue a 1099-NEC or equivalent tax form as required by the IRS.
  • Tax Information: You may be required to provide tax identification information (e.g., SSN, EIN, TIN, or equivalent) as part of the Creator onboarding process or at any time upon request.
  • Withholding: PNPtv may be required to withhold taxes on Creator payouts in certain jurisdictions. If withholding applies, it will be deducted from your earnings before payout.
  • No Tax Advice: PNPtv does not provide tax advice. We recommend consulting a qualified tax professional regarding your Creator earnings.

This includes displaying your content to paying subscribers and users, generating previews and thumbnails, and featuring your content in platform promotions.

6. Exclusive vs. Non-Exclusive Content

  • Non-Exclusive Content (Default): By default, all content uploaded to PNPtv is non-exclusive. You are free to post the same content on other platforms.
  • Exclusive Content: You may choose to designate specific content as "PNPtv Exclusive." Exclusive content is promoted more prominently on the platform and may receive enhanced discoverability. When you designate content as exclusive, you agree not to publish that same content on competing platforms for the duration of its exclusive status.
  • Removing Exclusivity: You may remove the exclusive designation from your content at any time through your Creator Dashboard.

7. Creator Obligations

As a Creator, you agree to:

  • Comply with all applicable laws and regulations in your jurisdiction.
  • Only upload content that you own or have the right to distribute.
  • Ensure all individuals appearing in your content are 18+ and have provided their documented consent.
  • Maintain records of age verification and consent for all individuals appearing in your content, as required by applicable law (including 18 U.S.C. 2257 or equivalent regulations).
  • Comply with PNPtv's Content Policy and Community Guidelines at all times.
  • Respond to platform communications in a timely manner.
  • Keep your payout information up to date.
  • Not engage in artificial inflation of engagement metrics (fake views, purchases, etc.).

8. Prohibited Creator Content

In addition to the restrictions in our Content Policy and Community Guidelines, Creators are specifically prohibited from:

  • Content involving minors in any capacity (zero tolerance, immediate permanent ban).
  • Non-consensual content of any kind.
  • Substance-related content restrictions: Creators may NOT (a) sell, distribute, traffic, or facilitate the sale of illegal substances; (b) ask viewers how or where to obtain illegal substances; (c) tell, teach, or instruct viewers on how to obtain, manufacture, or synthesize illegal substances. These restrictions apply to video content, captions, live streams, DMs, and any creator-to-fan interaction on the platform.
  • Props vs. real substances — mandatory declaration: If a Creator's video depicts substance use, the Creator MUST be able to declare on demand whether the depiction uses theatrical props or real substances. PNPtv strongly encourages prop use for performance content. Real-substance depictions, where shown, must comply with the substance-related rules above (no sourcing, no instructions, harm-reduction framing) and must not glorify reckless use. Failing to declare prop-vs-real on a moderator's request will result in the content being unpublished pending review.
  • Content that misleads users about what they are purchasing (bait-and-switch).
  • Reposting other Creators' content without their permission.
  • Using the platform to redirect users to external payment methods to circumvent PNPtv's payment system.

9. Termination of Creator Status

Creator status may be terminated under the following circumstances:

  • Voluntary: You may terminate your Creator status at any time by contacting support@pnptv.app. Any outstanding earnings above the minimum payout threshold will be paid out in the next regular payout cycle.
  • Policy Violation: PNPtv may terminate your Creator status immediately for violations of these terms, the Content Policy, or Community Guidelines. Severity determines whether outstanding earnings are forfeited.
  • Inactivity: Creator accounts with no new content and no earnings for 12 consecutive months may be reverted to standard user accounts. You will be notified before this occurs.
  • Fraud: Creator status will be immediately and permanently terminated for any fraudulent activity, including fake engagement, chargeback fraud, or identity misrepresentation. Outstanding earnings will be forfeited.

Upon termination of Creator status, your existing content remains on the platform unless you request its removal or your account is banned. You may request removal of all your Creator content by emailing support@pnptv.app.
