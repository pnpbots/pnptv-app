Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 28, 2026

PNPtv respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in strict compliance with the GDPR (Europe), CCPA/CPRA (USA), and Ley Estatutaria 1581 de 2012 (Colombia — Habeas Data).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Username, profile data, email, and authentication data from third-party login providers.
  • Usage Data: IP address, device information, browser type, and interaction logs.
  • Payment Data: Processed securely by third-party providers (e.g., ePayco, Meru). We do not store full credit card information.
  • Verification Data: Government-issued ID or biometric data specifically requested for age and 2257 compliance (stored securely and temporarily as required by law).

2. Legal Basis & How We Use Your Information

We process your data based on:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the Platform and process transactions.
  • Legitimate Interest: To prevent fraud, improve services, and maintain platform security.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with tax, age-verification, and criminal prevention laws.
  • Consent: For marketing communications and optional cookies.

3. Data Sharing & International Transfers

We may share data with payment processors, hosting providers, and legal authorities when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence (including Colombia and the USA). We ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place for international data transfers.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as your account is active, or as required by law (e.g., financial records, 2257 compliance data, and fraud prevention logs).

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the following rights:

Europe (GDPR)

  • The right to access, rectify, and erase your data (Right to be Forgotten)
  • The right to restrict and object to processing
  • The right to data portability

Colombia (Ley 1581 de 2012 — Habeas Data)

  • The right to know, update, and rectify your personal data
  • The right to request proof of authorization
  • The right to revoke consent

USA (CCPA/CPRA)

  • California residents have the right to know what data is collected
  • The right to request deletion of personal data
  • The right to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data

Note: PNPtv does not sell your data.

Requests to exercise these rights must be submitted to: legal@pnptv.app

6. Security & Cookies

We implement high-level technical and organizational measures to protect data, though no system is 100% secure.

We use cookies to maintain sessions and analyze usage. You may disable non-essential cookies via your browser or our cookie consent banner.

7. Modifications

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the updated version.
