Content Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

This Content Policy governs all user-generated content on PNPtv, including photos, videos, live streams, posts, messages, and profile information. PNPtv is an 18+ adult community platform. Adult content between consenting adults is permitted within the boundaries described below. This policy also includes our DMCA and copyright provisions.

1. Permitted Content

PNPtv is an adult community platform, and the following types of content are permitted:

  • Adult sexual content between consenting adults who are 18 years of age or older.
  • Nudity, intimate photos, and videos shared by or with the consent of the individuals depicted.
  • Live video streams featuring consenting adult participants.
  • Community discussions, social posts, and direct messages.
  • Personal profiles with photos, bios, and self-descriptions.
  • Content related to the PNP lifestyle, community events, and social connection.
  • Harm reduction discussions and educational content about safer practices.
  • Creator content offered through the platform's monetization features.

All permitted content must comply with these additional requirements:

  • All individuals depicted must be 18 years of age or older.
  • All individuals depicted must have consented to the creation and sharing of the content.
  • Content must be properly categorized and tagged according to platform categories.

2. Strictly Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly banned from PNPtv. Posting prohibited content will result in immediate content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban, and may be reported to law enforcement where required by law.

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any content that depicts, describes, or promotes the sexual exploitation of minors. This includes real, digitally generated, or illustrated content. Zero tolerance. Immediate permanent ban and report to NCMEC and applicable law enforcement.
  • Non-Consensual Intimate Content: Any intimate or sexual content shared without the consent of all individuals depicted. This includes revenge content, hidden camera recordings, and content shared beyond its intended audience without consent.
  • Non-Consensual Sexual Acts: Any content depicting sexual assault, rape, or any sexual act performed without the clear consent of all parties.
  • Illegal Substance Trafficking: Content that facilitates the sale, distribution, or trafficking of controlled substances. Note: PNPtv is a PNP community and personal lifestyle discussions in the context of harm reduction are permitted; however, the platform cannot be used as a marketplace for illegal substances.
  • Human Trafficking and Exploitation: Any content that promotes, facilitates, or advertises human trafficking, forced labor, or sexual exploitation.
  • Extreme Violence: Content depicting real acts of extreme violence, torture, gore, or content that glorifies real-world violence.
  • Terrorism: Content that promotes, supports, or glorifies terrorist organizations or acts of terrorism.
  • Animal Abuse: Content depicting sexual acts with animals or animal cruelty.
  • Doxxing Material: Content that exposes another person's private information (real name, address, phone number, workplace) without their consent.
  • Malware and Phishing: Content designed to deceive users into revealing personal information or installing malicious software.

4. Platform Moderation Rights

PNPtv reserves the right to review, moderate, and remove any content at our discretion. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Content that violates this Content Policy, our Community Guidelines, or our Terms of Service.
  • Content flagged by community reports that is determined to be in violation.

  • Content that is technically disruptive to the platform (e.g., excessively large files, spam).

We may use a combination of automated tools and human review to moderate content. Moderation decisions can be appealed by emailing legal@pnptv.app.

5. Reporting Violations

If you encounter content that violates this policy, please report it immediately:

  • In-App Reporting: Use the report button on any piece of content, profile, or message.
  • Email: Send a detailed report to support@pnptv.app including the content URL or screenshot, the username of the poster, and a description of the violation.
  • CSAM Reports: If you encounter any content involving minors, report it immediately to support@pnptv.app and to NCMEC CyberTipline.

We aim to review all reports within 24 hours. Reports involving minors or imminent safety threats are prioritized and reviewed immediately.

6. DMCA and Copyright

PNPtv respects intellectual property rights and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). If you believe that your copyrighted work has been copied or used on PNPtv in a way that constitutes copyright infringement, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice.

Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice:

To file a valid DMCA takedown notice, send a written notification to our designated agent that includes the following:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work(s) claimed to have been infringed.
  • Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, including its location on PNPtv (URL or sufficient detail to locate it).
  • Your contact information: name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf.

Designated DMCA Agent:

  • Name: PNPtv Legal Department
  • Email: legal@pnptv.app
  • Subject Line: DMCA Takedown Notice

Counter-Notification:

If you believe your content was removed in error due to a DMCA takedown notice, you may file a counter-notification by sending a written notice to our designated agent that includes:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the material that was removed and its location on PNPtv before removal.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  • Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court in your district and that you will accept service of process from the person who filed the original takedown notice.

Upon receiving a valid counter-notification, PNPtv will forward it to the original complainant and may restore the removed content within 10-14 business days unless the complainant notifies us that they have filed a court action.

Repeat Infringers:

PNPtv will terminate the accounts of users who are determined to be repeat copyright infringers. A user who receives three valid DMCA takedown notices may have their account permanently terminated.

7. Content Removal and Appeals

When content is removed for policy violations:

  • The content owner will be notified of the removal and the reason for it.
  • The content owner may appeal the decision by emailing legal@pnptv.app with "Content Appeal" in the subject line.
  • Appeals will be reviewed within 14 business days.
  • If the appeal is successful, the content will be restored. If denied, the decision is final.
