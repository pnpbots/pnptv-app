Refund Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds, cancellations, and disputes are handled for purchases made on PNPtv. We aim to be fair and transparent in all billing matters. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Digital Services - General Policy

PNPtv provides digital services including membership subscriptions, creator content, and platform features. Due to the nature of digital services, purchases are generally non-refundable once access has been granted. By completing a purchase, you acknowledge that:

  • You are purchasing access to digital content and services that are delivered immediately or upon the start of your billing cycle.
  • Once you have accessed the digital content or service, the transaction is considered complete.
  • Refund eligibility is determined on a case-by-case basis as described in this policy.

2. Subscription Refunds

For recurring subscription purchases (MEMBER and PRIME tiers):

  • Within 48 hours of initial purchase: If you have not significantly used premium features, you may request a full refund of your first subscription payment. Contact support@pnptv.app within 48 hours of your initial subscription purchase.
  • After 48 hours: Subscription payments are non-refundable. You may cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges (see Section 3).
  • Renewal charges: Automatic renewal charges are non-refundable. If you do not wish to be charged for the next billing cycle, you must cancel your subscription before the renewal date.
  • Billing errors: If you were charged incorrectly (e.g., double-charged, wrong amount), contact us immediately and we will issue a full refund for the erroneous charge.

3. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your PNPtv subscription at any time:

  • Cancellation can be done through your account settings or by emailing support@pnptv.app.
  • When you cancel, your subscription remains active until the end of your current paid billing period. You will not be charged for the next billing cycle.
  • You retain access to all premium features until your current billing period expires.
  • After your billing period expires, your account will revert to the Free tier.
  • Cancellation does not generate a prorated refund for the remaining days in your current billing period.
  • You may re-subscribe at any time at the then-current pricing.

4. Creator Content Purchases

For one-time purchases of creator content (tips, exclusive content, etc.):

  • Content not delivered: If you paid for creator content that was never delivered or was removed before you could access it, you are entitled to a full refund. Contact support@pnptv.app with proof of purchase.
  • Content as described: If the content was delivered and matches its description, the purchase is non-refundable.
  • Misrepresented content: If creator content was materially different from what was advertised or described, you may request a refund within 7 days of purchase. We will review the claim and make a determination.
  • Tips and donations: Tips and voluntary donations sent to creators are non-refundable.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  • Email support@pnptv.app with the subject line "Refund Request."
  • Include your PNPtv username or the email address associated with your account.
  • Include the date and amount of the charge you are requesting a refund for.
  • Include the reason for your refund request.
  • If applicable, include any supporting documentation (screenshots, transaction IDs).

We will acknowledge your refund request within 2 business days and provide a final determination within 7 business days.

6. Refund Processing

When a refund is approved:

  • Credit card payments (via ePayco): Refunds will be credited back to the original payment method. Processing time is typically 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
  • Stablecoin payments (via Daimo): Refunds for cryptocurrency payments will be returned to the original wallet address in the same stablecoin used for payment. Processing time is typically 1-3 business days.
  • Currency: Refunds are processed in the same currency as the original transaction. Exchange rate fluctuations are not our responsibility.

7. Chargebacks and Disputes

We strongly encourage you to contact us directly at support@pnptv.app before initiating a chargeback or dispute with your bank or payment provider. We are committed to resolving billing issues fairly and promptly.

  • Contact us first: Most issues can be resolved quickly through our support team. Chargebacks are costly and time-consuming for all parties.
  • Fraudulent chargebacks: Filing a chargeback for a legitimate transaction that was properly delivered is considered fraud. PNPtv reserves the right to suspend accounts associated with fraudulent chargebacks and to pursue recovery of funds.
  • Dispute resolution: If you have initiated a chargeback, we will provide evidence of the transaction to your payment provider. Your account may be suspended pending resolution of the dispute.
  • Account reinstatement: If a chargeback is resolved in our favor, you will be required to settle the outstanding balance before your account can be reinstated.

8. Exceptional Circumstances

We may issue refunds outside of the standard policy in exceptional circumstances, including:

  • Extended platform outages that prevented you from accessing paid services for a significant portion of your billing period.
  • Account termination by PNPtv where the user was not at fault (e.g., a moderation error later reversed).
  • Legal or regulatory requirements.

Exceptional refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis at PNPtv's discretion.

9. Account Termination and Refunds

  • Voluntary account deletion: If you delete your account, no refund is issued for the remaining subscription period. Cancel your subscription first and wait for the billing period to end before deleting your account.
  • Account banned for policy violations: If your account is banned for violating our Community Guidelines or Terms of Service, no refund will be issued.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. The policy in effect at the time of your purchase applies to that transaction.

11. Contact

For refund requests, billing questions, or disputes:

  • Support: support@pnptv.app
  • Legal: legal@pnptv.app
