Last updated: March 4, 2026

These Subscription Terms govern all paid membership plans on PNPtv. By subscribing to any paid tier, you agree to these terms in addition to our Terms of Service and Refund Policy.

1. Membership Tiers

PNPtv offers the following membership tiers:

  • Free: Basic access to the platform at no cost. Includes limited community features, public profiles, and basic messaging. Free accounts are subject to feature limitations compared to paid tiers.
  • MEMBER ($9.99/month): Enhanced access including expanded community features, access to member-only content, advanced messaging, participation in live video rooms, and priority support.
  • PRIME ($14.99 - $249.99): Premium access with all MEMBER features plus exclusive content, advanced creator tools, priority placement, extended storage, and additional premium features. PRIME is available in multiple durations (monthly, quarterly, annually, and lifetime) at varying price points.

Detailed feature comparisons for each tier are available on our pricing page within the app.

2. Billing and Payment

When you subscribe to a paid tier:

  • Billing cycle: Subscriptions are billed at the beginning of each billing period (monthly, quarterly, or annually, depending on the plan selected).
  • First charge: Your first payment is processed immediately upon subscribing. Access to paid features is granted as soon as payment is confirmed.
  • Auto-renewal: All subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing period at the then-current rate, unless you cancel before the renewal date. You will be charged using the same payment method on file.
  • Billing date: Your billing date is set to the day you initially subscribed. For example, if you subscribe on March 15, you will be billed on the 15th of each subsequent month (for monthly plans).

3. Payment Methods

PNPtv accepts the following payment methods:

  • Credit and Debit Cards (via ePayco): We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and other major card networks through our payment processor ePayco. All card transactions are processed securely and comply with PCI DSS standards.
  • Cryptocurrency (Dash via BTCPay): We accept payments in Dash through the BTCPay self-hosted payment network. Dash payments are processed on-chain and typically confirm within 2-5 minutes thanks to InstantSend.

Currency: All prices are listed in US Dollars (USD). Credit card payments processed through ePayco may be converted to Colombian Pesos (COP) at the time of transaction, as ePayco is a Colombia-based payment processor. Your card issuer may apply its own exchange rate and foreign transaction fees. Dash payments are quoted in USD and converted to Dash at the live market rate at the time of checkout.

PNPtv reserves the right to change subscription prices. When prices change:

  • We will provide at least 30 days' advance notice before any price increase takes effect.
  • Notice will be sent via email and/or in-app notification to all active subscribers.
  • Price changes will apply to your next billing cycle after the notice period has elapsed.
  • If you do not agree with a price change, you may cancel your subscription before the new price takes effect. Your current billing period will remain at the old price.
  • Promotional or introductory pricing is for the specified promotional period only. After the promotional period, standard pricing applies.

5. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time:

  • How to cancel: Go to your account settings and select "Manage Subscription," then follow the cancellation steps. Alternatively, email support@pnptv.app with your cancellation request.
  • Effective date: Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You will retain access to all paid features until that date.
  • No partial refunds: We do not issue prorated refunds for unused portions of a billing period. See our Refund Policy for details.
  • Confirmation: You will receive an email confirmation of your cancellation with the date your access will revert to the Free tier.

6. Downgrades

If you downgrade from a higher tier to a lower tier (e.g., PRIME to MEMBER, or any paid tier to Free):

  • Your higher-tier access continues until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • At the end of the billing period, your account will transition to the new tier with its corresponding features and limitations.
  • Content you created while on a higher tier will remain, but access to certain premium features associated with the higher tier will be restricted.
  • If your storage usage exceeds the limits of the lower tier, you will not be able to upload new content until your usage is within the new tier's limits. Existing content will not be deleted.

7. Upgrades

If you upgrade from a lower tier to a higher tier:

  • Access to the higher tier's features is granted immediately upon successful payment.
  • If you upgrade mid-cycle, you will be charged the prorated difference for the remaining days in your current billing period, plus the full amount for the new tier starting from your next billing date.
  • Your billing date remains the same.

  • During the retry period, your subscription remains active.
  • If all retry attempts fail, your subscription will be suspended, and your account will revert to the Free tier.
  • You can reactivate your subscription at any time by updating your payment method and completing a successful payment.
